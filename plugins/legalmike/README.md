# LegalMike plugin

Research Dutch and EU law from primary sources in Claude or ChatGPT, with a
working link to every ruling and statute cited.

The plugin connects to the LegalMike service at `https://mcp.legalmike.ai/mcp`
(sign-in with a LegalMike account) and adds two skills: `legal-research`, which
researches a question and presents the answer with every source linked, and
`check-citations`, which checks the citations and claims in a draft against
the sources. Answers are legal research for review by a qualified lawyer, not
legal advice.

More: https://github.com/LegalMikeAI/legalmike-plugins · https://legalmike.ai
